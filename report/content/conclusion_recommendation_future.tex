\chapter{Conclusion, recommendation, and future work}
\label{ch:conclusion-recommendation-future}
This project successfully designed and implemented an MLOps pipeline for predicting chess game outcomes. This can be valuable work for further research and development in chess analytics and chess computer research. The pipeline can be extended to support more complex models, such as deep learning architectures, and to incorporate additional features, such as player behavior patterns and psychological factors. Future work could also explore real-time prediction during live games, which would require optimizing the pipeline for low latency and high throughput. Additionally, the insights generated from the model could be used to develop personalized training programs for chess players, helping them to improve their skills and strategies based on their unique playing styles and weaknesses.
